Beauty is

The Great Red Spot is a storm that has raged for at least 350 years, wide enough to swallow Earth whole. Jupiter's gravity shapes the entire outer system.

08 / Neptune

The edge
of the map.

Winds of 2,100 km/h — the fastest recorded in the solar system — tear across a planet so far out its year lasts 165 of ours. Farthest, coldest, last.

Diameter49,244 km
Fastest winds2,100 km/h
Known moons16
Field notes

Worlds within
worlds.

Io
Moon of Jupiter
The most volcanically active body in the solar system, resurfaced constantly by Jupiter's gravitational squeeze.
Europa
Moon of Jupiter
An ice shell over a liquid ocean, and one of the best bets for finding life beyond Earth.
Titan
Moon of Saturn
Rivers and lakes of liquid methane under a thick orange haze — the only moon with a substantial atmosphere.
Enceladus
Moon of Saturn
Geysers of water ice erupt from its south pole, hinting at a hidden ocean beneath a frozen crust.
Triton
Moon of Neptune
Orbits backwards — almost certainly a captured object from the icy Kuiper Belt, slowly spiraling inward.
The Moon
Moon of Earth
Formed from debris after a Mars-sized body struck early Earth. It's drifting away, 3.8 cm a year.
